Your credentials do not become our data.

  • API credentials stay in the macOS Keychain — they never leave your machine.
  • No backend, no telemetry, no account. Everything reads local files on disk.
  • Open source — read every line before you run it.
  • The one network call is an optional 1-token probe to Anthropic, and you can turn it off.
